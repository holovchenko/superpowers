### When the suite has failures outside your edit scope

If your fix makes the target behavior pass but the suite is still red because of
**pre-existing failures unrelated to your change and outside your locked edit surface**:

1. Do NOT widen your edit surface to fix them, and do NOT stop to ask the human.
2. Diagnose spec-vs-scope-vs-test: confirm the failures are pre-existing and out of scope.
3. Prove no-regression: capture the baseline fail-count (stash/move-aside your change and
   re-run, or compare against the known baseline) and show your change does not increase it
   — your target test flips red→green, every other failure count is unchanged.
4. Amend the gate: record that "whole suite green" is unreachable in-scope and re-scope the
   gate to "target test green AND no NEW failures vs baseline" (write a one-line amendment).
5. Proceed as resolved once the amended gate is met. Do not block on the human for an
   out-of-scope baseline you were not asked to fix.
